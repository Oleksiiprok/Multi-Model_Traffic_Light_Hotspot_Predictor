## Multi-Model Traffic Light Hotspot Predictor
Multi-Model Traffic Light Hotspot Predictor is an interactive, web-based analytical tool designed for Google Colab and Jupyter Notebook environments. It utilizes machine learning algorithms to predict traffic congestion and accident risks at signalized intersections across several western suburbs of Chicago 
(Riverside, Brookfield, North Riverside, La Grange, Berwyn, and Lyons).

</div>
Key Features
Multi-Model AI Selection: 

Choose between three powerful machine learning algorithms dynamically: Granular Environmental & Traffic Controls: Adjust parameters in real-time using intuitive widgets:
Time of day (Hour slider) & Day of week;
Weather conditions (Temperature & Precipitation categories);
Traffic volume (Vehicles per hour) & Roadwork status toggle.

Flexible Temporal Filtering: 
Filter analytical datasets and retrain models on the fly by specific periods 
(All periods, 2025/2026 yearly splits, Winter, or Summer seasons).

Comprehensive AI Insights Report: 
Instantaneous statistical summaries detailing model pros, constraints, predicted congestion probability, and practical applications for intelligent traffic light scheduling.

Stable Layout & Design: A compact, two-column responsive layout with an adapted map container that seamlessly integrates control widgets, analytical reports, and geographic visualizers.
XGBoost: High predictive accuracy, perfectly capturing complex non-linear feature interactions.

Random Forest: 
Robust performance, highly resilient against noise and outliers in tabular data.

Logistic Regression:
Fast inference, lightweight, and provides a clear baseline for linear trends.

Interactive Suburb & Intersection Mapping: 
Select from 6 major suburbs and drill down into specific signalized intersections. The interactive Folium map displays risk-coded markers (Green, Orange, Red) with customized tooltips and popups.
